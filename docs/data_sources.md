# Kisan Shakti — Data Sources & Attribution

All datasets and institutional resources used in Kisan Shakti are documented, verifiable, and respected according to their respective public access terms.

---

## 1. Crop Disease Images (PlantVillage)
- **Source**: Hughes, D., & Salathe, M. (2015). "An open access repository of images on plant health to enable the development of mobile disease diagnostics." *arXiv:1511.08060*.
- **License**: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain).
- **Supported Subset**:
  - **Tomato**: Healthy, Early Blight, Late Blight, Bacterial Spot, Leaf Mold, Septoria Leaf Spot, Spider Mites, Target Spot, Yellow Leaf Curl Virus, Mosaic Virus.
  - **Potato**: Healthy, Early Blight, Late Blight.
  - **Corn (Maize)**: Healthy, Common Rust, Northern Leaf Blight, Gray Leaf Spot.
- **Limitation Notice**: PlantVillage images are largely collected under uniform laboratory lighting against neutral backgrounds. Accuracy on real farm fields with shadows, soil backgrounds, or complex ambient lighting requires local calibration.

## 2. Agricultural Knowledge Base & Advisories
- **Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR)**: `https://icar.org.in`
- **ICAR-Indian Institute of Horticultural Research (IIHR), Bengaluru**: Technical bulletins on tomato and vegetable diseases.
- **ICAR-Central Potato Research Institute (CPRI), Shimla**: Late blight forecasting protocols and disease management manuals.
- **ICAR-Indian Institute of Maize Research (IIMR), Ludhiana**: Fall Armyworm and foliar blight packages of practice.
- **ICAR-National Bureau of Agricultural Insect Resources (NBAIR), Bengaluru**: Biocontrol and pheromone trap recommendations.
